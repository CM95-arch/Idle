Rules this sheet assumes

  • Biomes (loot tiers): Forest 1–3 · Plains 2–4 · Desert 3–6 · Sprite Forest 4–7 · Abandoned City 5–8 · Hell 6–9 · Abyss 7–10 · Godlands 8–11.
  • Potions drop in Hell, Abyss and Godlands. The best gear (T12–T14 and UTs) only comes from dungeons.
  • Gear tiers: weapons and armor T1–T14. Abilities and rings follow ROTMG's shorter tier range.
  • Classes: Wizard, Priest, Archer, Rogue and Warrior from the start. Knight, Paladin, Huntress, Sorcerer, Necromancer and Assassin unlock through play.
  • Essence is the only currency. You get it by dismantling extra copies and surplus potions. You spend it on Forge (expensive), one rune, and cosmetics.
  • No offline cap. Death stops farming but loses nothing. Death in a dungeon loses that run's rewards.

Open questions

  • Retreat: should leaving a dungeon early keep the loot so far, like ROTMG, or count as falling?
  • Class unlocks: by reaching a level on another class, ROTMG-style (e.g. Knight from Warrior Lv 10)? Even with levels as score only, that still works.
  • Dungeon loot tiers and the full item list are waiting on your tier sheet.
